# Week 16: Capstone Project — Tutorial

## Why this week matters
This is the project that goes on your resume and LinkedIn. It should prove,
in one place, that you can take a real problem from raw data to a deployed,
working product.

---

## 1. Choosing a Real Problem

Avoid another Titanic/Iris-style toy dataset — pick something where:
- The problem isn't already solved a thousand times on Kaggle
- You had to make real decisions about scope, data collection, and tradeoffs
- You can explain *why it matters*, not just *how it works*
